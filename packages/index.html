7-Stage Payment Milestones

Pay step-by-step as work progresses on site with complete peace of mind and full financial security.

01
Advance & Agreement

20% Milestone

Agreement signing, architectural designs, 3D renderings, structural calculations, and municipal approval paperwork.

02
Substructure

15% Milestone

Site preparation, excavation, footings, foundation column bases, and plinth beam concreting.

03
Structure & Slab

20% Milestone

Reinforced concrete columns, beams, staircases, and ground/first floor slab casting.

04
Masonry & Roof

15% Milestone

Complete brickwork wall masonry, lintels, roof truss framing, and tile/sheet roof installation.

05
Plaster & Services

15% Milestone

Internal/external wall plastering, electrical conduit wiring, and plumbing hot/cold pipe laying.

06
Finishing Phase

10% Milestone

Floor tiling, ceiling fitting, door/window frame installation, bathroom sanitaryware, and painting.

07
Handover & Testing

5% Final Milestone

Final quality inspections, water/electrical testing, deep cleaning, key handover, and structural warranty certificate release.
